Base rule: clause 12.1, page 32. Minimum overall attendance 75%.
  65% to below 75%: make-up mid-exam and end-semester exam with grade cap B+.
  Below 65%: 'R' grade.
Condonation: clause 13 and 13.2, pages 33-34. Shortage may be condoned by 10% (down to 65%)
  for medical emergency or representing the university/country.
  13.2: Vice-Chancellor decides on recommendation of Principal/Director and remarks
  from the Medical officer.
Process (page 34): documents from registered medical practitioner -> Mentor -> HoI -> medical officer.
Possible conflicts: 12.1 vs 12.2/13 (whether 65-75% students may sit the exam);
  regulations_3.1.pdf says below 75% is not permitted (check which programmes it covers).

Expected citations: clause 12.1 (base rule) and clause 13.2 (condonation).
